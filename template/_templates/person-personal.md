# Personal context — First Last

> Owner/partner only. This captures the **person's own drivers** — separate from the company's
> goals in `CONTEXT.md`. Kept apart on purpose: personal drivers and business facts change at
> different speeds, and holding them side by side is what lets `/kb-align` spot the **tensions**
> between what you *want* and what you're *doing*. Write honestly; nobody but owners/partners reads this.

## 🎯 Why — what this business is ultimately for (in your life)
> The real prize. Not revenue — what the money/venture buys you.
- e.g. a house · more time with family · freedom to choose my work

## 🏁 What "enough" looks like (the finish line)
> So we can notice when work overshoots the point of it. Make it concrete.
- Income/assets: …  · Lifestyle: …  · "I've made it when …"

## 🧭 Values & non-negotiables (won't trade for success)
- e.g. health · being present for my kids · not becoming the bottleneck · integrity with clients

## ⏳ Time & energy (honest budget)
- Realistic hours/week I *want* to work: …
- Hard boundaries: e.g. weekends off · no work after 19:00 · X weeks holiday/year
- I work best when: …  · What drains me: …

## 🎭 The role I actually want to play
> Visionary / operator / executor / investor. Big source of the "wants autonomy but does the work" tension.
- I want to spend my time on: …
- I want to stop doing: …

## 🌱 Life priority this season (outside the business)
- e.g. new baby · moving house · training for a marathon · caring for a parent

## 🚫 Red lines — must never happen
- e.g. burnout again · missing my kids growing up · debt I can't cover · losing a key client's trust

## 💰 Money & security — the personal "why" behind the numbers
- What I need to feel financially safe: …  · What money is *for*: …

- …
